## Direction contract

THESIS: The automotive-CGI studio standard, played straight at the craft of Fuze, Unit Image, The Mill and Platige: the film owns the screen, the interface is a slim bar, the works are a hover-to-play wall. The one thing taken from the subject: every film keeps its true frame, 2.39:1, 9:16 or 1:1. Refuses invented worlds, cards, ornament, and the uniform 16:9 grid.

OWN-WORLD: warm near-black #0c0a08 ground, #181512 for the second ground as a tone step, white ink, #8f8a84 only for secondary notes, gold #c9a45a only as a link hover (owner, 2026-10-26: a portfolio, not a sales page, so no Start a project button). Unbounded 800 uppercase display, Onest 400/500 text (Latin and Cyrillic), white ULTEMA wordmark as the only logo. Square corners, no rules, no shadows at rest, no gradients beyond two neutral scrims on the reel: one under the hero type, one under the bar so its white links stay legible over bright frames (accessibility, finish review 2026-10-26). The display email is Unbounded 300 lowercase: an address set at display size reads only in the light weight, and capitals would garble it (DESIGN.md "address" role).

STORY: the reel says cinematic car CGI by one person within seconds → the wall shows fifteen films in their real frames, hover plays each, filter by frame → a film opens its own page with its materials (Sky Race: storyboard to stills) → the name and who he is (CG generalist; Unreal Engine, Blender, Cinema 4D, EmberGen) beside a round portrait (owner) → the email as display type.

FIRST VIEWPORT: owner-pinned after The Mill (2026-10-26): first load opens on black with the ULTEMA letters cut out at 62vw and the reel playing through them; after 1.5s the letters open to the full screen and the logo appears top-left in the slim bar. Full-bleed muted showreel at 100svh (16:9 desktop, 9:16 portrait phones), neutral scrims under the bar and under the copy.
