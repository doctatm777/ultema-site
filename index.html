What I do

Services

01

Full-CG commercials

Concept, storyboard, modeling, animation, lighting and final render in Unreal Engine 5. Ролики полностью в CG — без съёмок.

02

VFX & simulations

Smoke, fire, contrails, volumetric clouds, particles — EmberGen, Niagara, VDB. Реалистичные симуляции для кино и рекламы.

03

Compositing

Integration of CG into live-action, clean-up, look-dev and grading. Композитинг для рекламы и короткого метра.

04

Automotive CGI

High-poly car builds with custom wheels, aero kits and materials — glossy, matte, carbon. Авто-CGI для брендов и ивентов.

05

Previz & storyboard

Hand-drawn storyboards, blocking and camera previz before production. Раскадровка и превиз до старта продакшна.

06

AI-assisted pipeline

Fast mood boards, concept exploration and texture generation with AI tools where it speeds things up без потери качества.
